# Open Questions for Tax Counsel and Practitioner Review

**Status:** Working draft for Session 2 pre-read. Each item below is intended to be filed as an Open Question issue in the wg-tax repository using the intake template, where review sign-ups happen in comments.
**Compiled by:** Joseph Hughes Jr, from the working group's regulatory research. Edited for circulation.

Numbering continues the open-issues register in the strategy document, which runs OI-1 through OI-34.

These are the unresolved architectural and regulatory frictions extracted from the research annex. None of them can be settled by the working group alone; each requires practitioner, counsel, or regulator validation. Resolution of an item produces either a Requirement issue, a documented non-requirement, or a referral.

| **OI-36** | Settlement asset classification | **Synthetic stablecoin contamination.** Under the GENIUS Act (enacted 18 July 2025; effective on the earlier of 18 January 2027 or 120 days after the primary federal regulators issue final rules, which remain at proposed-rule stage) and the concurrent proposed FASB ASU, how must an enterprise merchant automatically segregate inbound x402 payments settled in non-PPSI synthetic wrappers (e.g., USDe) versus compliant PPSI tokens (e.g., USDC)? | Accounting guidance mapping: a GAAP workflow that distinguishes cash-equivalent stablecoin receipts from volatile intangible property receipts at scale. |

| **OI-38** | Nonprofit and philanthropic | **Charitable micro-aggregation paradox.** For a 501(c)(3) receiving thousands of autonomous sub-$1.00 x402 donations, does an annual, protocol-generated cryptographic giving summary legally satisfy the IRC §170(f)(8) requirement for a Contemporaneous Written Acknowledgment? | Legal review of precedent in donor-advised funds and digital batch processing to validate the aggregation methodology. |
| **OI-39** | Disputes and cross-period events | **Arbitral cross-period reversals.** If a tribunal operating under smart-contract arbitration rules orders an on-chain refund in Q4 of a transaction delivered and taxed in Q3, how does the resource server execute the VAT or sales tax reclaim without triggering an audit flag? | Accounting guidance mapping the subsequent-events doctrine to standard ERP credit memo generation across fiscal periods. |
| **OI-40** | Evidence and admissibility | **Signed request binding as business-records evidence.** Will a court or arbitrator accept a request-bound signature (ERC-8128, Signed HTTP Requests with Ethereum, built on RFC 9421 HTTP Message Signatures) as definitive proof of intent, defeating an "unauthorized agent" or prompt-injection defense, without accompanying human forensic testimony? | Legal stress-test of the evidence bundle against the FRE 803(6) business-records exception and the computer-generated versus computer-stored records distinction. |
| **OI-41** | Delegation and attribution | **Authority laundering in sub-operators.** When an enterprise agent autonomously delegates to a third-party micro-agent platform, does the original principal retain tax and liability attribution for downstream x402 actions, or does the orchestration platform become the reseller or merchant of record? | Legal review producing a factor test for allocating liability within nested agent-to-agent delegation chains. |

## Relationship to the principles

Several items are the principles operating on specific regulatory planes, which is worth naming when they're walked in session. OI-35 is Principle 5 on the reporting plane: a compliance anchor (aggregationId) potentially creating an obligation (broker status) is precisely a hidden role transition. OI-37 is Principle 4's protected-disclosure mechanism meeting a documentary-evidence statute. OI-39 exercises Principle 6's append-only, as-of-commit-time record model across fiscal periods. OI-40 and OI-42 test whether Principle 6 and 7 records do legal work under adversarial conditions. OI-41 is Principle 5 on the liability plane.
